## 1. Who We Are

Feliciterra ("the Network," "we," "us," "the family," or "whoever is on call this weekend") operates the services and applications available at **feliciterra.com** and **feliciterra.net**. The Network is a private, family-operated infrastructure maintained by and for members of the Turney family.

If you found this page by accident, welcome. Please read Section 2 immediately.

---

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to **authorized users of the Feliciterra network** — that is, members of the Turney family and individuals who have been expressly granted access by a network administrator (i.e., a Turney who knows what they're doing, or at minimum one who is confident they do).

If you are not a Turney family member or otherwise authorized user, you have no lawful basis for accessing any service hosted at feliciterra.com or feliciterra.net. Kindly close this tab, reflect on the choices that led you here, and have a lovely day.

---

## 3. What Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate our services. This may include:

- **Account information** you provide when registering for a service (name, email address, the nickname only your family uses for you)
- **Usage data** such as server logs, access timestamps, and error reports — the sort of thing that helps us figure out why the service went down at 11 PM on a Friday
- **Authentication credentials** managed through our identity providers — passwords are hashed, because we took at least one security course
- **Cookies** — the digital kind. They help keep you logged in and the applications functional. They are not as good as the cookies Grandma makes, but they serve their purpose.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the services you access on the Network
- Authenticate your identity and ensure you are, in fact, a Turney (or authorized friend thereof)
- Diagnose technical issues and restore service after the inevitable homelab incident
- Contact you regarding important service updates (read: "the server is down again, we're working on it")

We do not sell your information. We do not share your information with third parties except where strictly required to operate the services (e.g., cloud providers, authentication platforms). We do not use your information to serve you advertisements. This is a family network, not a data brokerage.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on infrastructure maintained within the Feliciterra network, including servers and storage systems at feliciterra.net. We implement reasonable security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and periodic reminders to update things.

"Reasonable security practices" for a homelab means we try our best. We are not a Fortune 500 company with a dedicated security team. We are a family with strong opinions about self-hosting.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you wish to have your data removed, please contact the network administrator — which is to say, please message the family group chat. Someone will get to it.
